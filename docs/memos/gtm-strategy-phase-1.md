# GTM Strategy: Phase 1 — "The Singapore AI Wedge"

Expanding the Help & Grow ecosystem through a focused, high-value cohort in the SEA region.

---

## High-Level Vision
Help & Grow is the **AI Native Expert Network**. We avoid the "marketplace" label to emphasize mutual growth. Phase 1 targets the **Singapore AI Founder/Investor community** as our primary wedge.

---

## 1. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) Definition
**ICP** stands for **Ideal Customer Profile**. It is the specific persona that derives the most immediate value from our network and has the highest likelihood of becoming an evangelist.

**Phase 1 ICP**:
- **Role**: AI Startup Founders, Seed/Series A Investors, and Regional VPs of Expansion.
- **Geography**: Primarily **Singapore**, catering to "everyone doing business in Singapore"—including those expanding from HK, HK, and Silicon Valley.
- **Pain Point**: High-speed networking with domain expert verified (via EAS/POMP) and finding localized support for regional expansion.

---

## 2. Pricing & Liquidity Strategy
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Paid-Only Launch**: Based on stakeholder feedback, we are pivoting away from Free sessions. All Phase 1 sessions will be **Paid-Only** to establish a high-value signal from day one.

- **Baseline Pricing**: $30 - $100 SGD per 30-minute session (set by expert).
- **Redemption**: 100 H&G Tokens = $1 SGD discount at checkout.
- **Value Prop**: "Expert-Level advice is worth paying for." This attracts high-tier experts who would otherwise ignore a "volunteer" platform.

---

## 3. The "Share my profile" Feature
To facilitate growth, experts need to "own" their reputation outside our platform.

**Strategic Goal**: Turn every expert's profile into a **Telegram Marketing Asset**.
- **The Asset**: A rich, interactive Telegram card that can be shared in private groups and VCC lists.
- **The Design**: Optimized for "Social Contrast"—showing verified badges and active session counts.

---

## 4. Distribution Channels
1. **Telegram Groups**: Direct outreach in SG-based "AI Founders" and "Technopreneurship" groups.
2. **Referral Wedge**: Invite-only onboarding where top experts nominate peers.
3. **LinkedIn Social Proof**: Automatic "Reputation Dashboard" updates exported to LinkedIn posts.

---

## 5. Metrics of Success (Phase 1)
- **Primary**: 50 active SG-based experts in AI/B2B domains.
- **Secondary**: 200 "Paid Sessions" completed using the tRPC booking flow.
- **Retention**: % of learners who book a second expert within 30 days.
